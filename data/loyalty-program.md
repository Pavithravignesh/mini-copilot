# Acme Rewards Loyalty Program

## Tiers
There are three tiers: Silver, Gold and Platinum. Every customer starts at Silver. Gold requires $500 of spend in a calendar year. Platinum requires $1,500 of spend in a calendar year.

## Earning points
Silver members earn 1 point per $1 spent. Gold members earn 1.5 points per $1. Platinum members earn 2 points per $1. Points are added 14 days after delivery, once the return window has closed for most items.

## Redeeming points
100 points are worth $1 off a future order. Points can cover up to 50% of an order's value. Points cannot be redeemed for gift cards.

## Point expiry
Points expire 12 months after they are earned if the account has no purchase in that time. Any purchase resets the 12-month timer for all points.

## Tier benefits
Gold: free return shipping and early access to sales. Platinum: free return shipping, free express delivery and a dedicated support line.
