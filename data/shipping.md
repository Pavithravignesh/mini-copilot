# Acme Store Shipping Guide

## Delivery options
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and costs $5.99. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $14.99. Same-day delivery is available in North and Central regions for orders placed before 11 AM, for $19.99.

## Free shipping
Orders over $50 get free standard delivery. Platinum loyalty members get free express delivery on every order.

## Order tracking
Customers receive a tracking link by email and SMS once the order ships. Tracking updates every 4 hours.

## Delays
If an order is more than 3 days late, customers can request a shipping fee refund through the help center.
