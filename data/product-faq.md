# Acme Store Product FAQ

## Warranty
All electronics include a 1-year manufacturer warranty. An extended 2-year warranty can be bought at checkout for 10% of the item price.

## Price match
Acme matches the price of major online retailers within 7 days of purchase. The item must be identical and in stock at the other retailer.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are available from $10 to $500. They never expire and can be used online or in store.

## Payment methods
Acme accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and pay-later plans for orders above $100.
