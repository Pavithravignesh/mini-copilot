# Acme Store Returns Policy

## Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics have a shorter window of 15 days. Items bought during the annual December sale can be returned until January 31.

## Condition of items
Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals. Opened software, gift cards and personal care products cannot be returned.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives the item. Store credit is issued instantly if the customer prefers it, and comes with a 5% bonus.

## Return shipping
Return shipping is free for Gold and Platinum loyalty members. Other customers pay a flat fee of $4.99, which is deducted from the refund. Damaged or wrong items are always returned free of charge.
